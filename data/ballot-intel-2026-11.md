### HIGH confidence — build demo content around these
1. **Fremont city charter measure** — asks voters whether Fremont becomes a charter
   city (council–city manager system formalized; term limits 2→3 consecutive terms).
   Timeline verified: charter advisory committee → council vote July 28 → to ROV by
   Aug 7 → on November ballot. $610k–$855k to place on ballot (staff estimate).
   Sources:
   - https://tricityvoice.com/fremont-moves-forward-with-charter-city-plans/
   - https://eastbayinsiders.substack.com/p/fremont-charter-city-plan-heads-to
   - https://bayeast.org/wp-content/uploads/GovernmentAffairs20260605.pdf
2. **Measure RTM — Regional Transit Measure** (SB 63 / Connect Bay Area Act) —
   0.5% sales tax in Alameda/Contra Costa/San Mateo/Santa Clara (1% SF), 14 years,
   ~$980M/yr for BART/Caltrain/Muni/AC Transit/VTA/SamTrans as federal COVID aid
   runs out. On every Bay Area voter's ballot including Fremont.
   Sources:
   - https://ktvu.com/election/election-2026-california-voter-guide
   - https://github.com/obelix74/skills/blob/HEAD/ca-voter-guide/SKILL.md
3. **10+ statewide initiatives**, incl. the "Billionaire's Tax" (5% one-time tax on
   $1B+ assets for health care; Sanders/SEIU support).
   Source: https://ktvu.com/election/election-2026-california-voter-guide
